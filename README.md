Heart Disease Detection Analysis using Python
Objective:
The primary objective of this heart disease analysis project is to gain valuable insights into the factors contributing to heart disease and develop predictive tools for its early detection. The first step involves thoroughly understanding the dataset, identifying its structure, and addressing issues like missing or erroneous data through preprocessing techniques. This ensures the dataset is clean, accurate, and ready for further analysis. Exploratory data analysis (EDA) follows, where trends and patterns are identified. This includes examining demographic trends such as age and gender, analyzing the impact of risk factors like cholesterol levels, blood pressure, and maximum heart rate, and understanding the influence of categorical variables like chest pain type and exercise-induced angina.

Data Review:
Excel file contains data related to heart health, with 918 rows and 12 columns. Here's a overview of the columns:
Age: Age of the individual (numeric).
Gender: Gender (Male/Female).
ChestPainType: Type of chest pain (e.g., ATA, NAP, ASY, etc.).
RestingBP: Resting blood pressure (mm Hg).
Cholesterol: Cholesterol level (mg/dL).
FastingBS: Fasting blood sugar (>120 mg/dL, 1 = true, 0 = false).
RestingECG: Resting electrocardiographic results (e.g., Normal, ST, etc.).
MaxHR: Maximum heart rate achieved.
ExerciseAngina: Exercise-induced angina (Y/N).
Oldpeak: Depression induced by exercise relative to rest.
ST_Slope: Slope of the peak exercise ST segment (e.g., Up, Flat).
HeartDisease: Target variable indicating the presence of heart disease (1 = disease, 0 = no disease).

Conclusion:
 The dataset shows that individuals across a wide age range are represented, with a noticeable clustering in middle-aged groups, suggesting that heart-related conditions are prevalent in this demographic.Males seem to have a higher prevalence of heart disease compared to females. This aligns with known medical observations that men are at a higher risk of heart conditions in certain age groups.
